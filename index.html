Our mission

A warm meal can be the beginning of hope.

Let's BEET the Hunger exists to give back, strengthen our community, and help ensure that people experiencing hunger are treated with dignity, compassion, and respect.

We are not a food pantry. Fresh meals are prepared through MeMe's Twisted Potato and More LLC, packaged, loaded into our personal vehicle, and distributed directly throughout Little Rock and North Little Rock.

We believe meeting people where they are makes support more immediate, personal, and meaningful.

🌱

Why the beet?

Nourishment, growth, and resilience

The beet represents nourishment, strength, and the ability to grow in difficult conditions. Our name reflects our commitment to fighting hunger one fresh meal at a time while cultivating hope and stronger communities.

Our story

Giving back through food, service, and direct outreach

Meredith Coleman and Chris Harding founded Let's BEET the Hunger to give back and better their community by helping ensure that no one feels forgotten.

Working together through MeMe's Twisted Potato and More LLC, fresh meals are prepared, packaged, loaded into their personal vehicle, and delivered directly throughout Little Rock and North Little Rock.

A warm meal can provide more than nourishment. It can offer encouragement, human connection, and a reminder that someone cares.
